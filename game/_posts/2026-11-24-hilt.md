## Thoughts on HILT, the reprint of Condottiere

I played [**HILT**](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/484019/hilt) yesterday, the reprint of the great game Condottiere. I shouldn't have played it with that group because the game is fragile enough, and the group did not care for games where you have to negotiate. iIn the teach I spent about 20 seconds repeating that the game is difficult and almost impossible to win unless you work with someone. Instead of organically subtle turns and colluding with one another, nobody felt like it was worth working together. The immediate consequence of this was that the winner of the game felt randomly decided (because they had the best hand) and the usual dramatic end to the game was anticlimactic and "obvious".     

What should have been the first round introduction into a guaranteed runback was followed by suggestions to play the new euro hotness [**Kingdom Crossing**](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/437245/kingdom-crossing). I have nothing against the new euro hotness, this is just to juxtapose the two games. After that game I had a long ponder about whether HILT is good or not. A great game should be able to convince a person that it is at least fun. A great game should not immediately test the patience of the players. I just finished ranking every single game I ever played with about 60% of games I played being rated 5 or lower. A 5 to me is completely average, so a game rated 5.8 is "almost good". Condottiere hits a 7.8 putting it at "great game" territory. If someone on the street asked me "hey how do you feel about Condottiere I'd say yeah that's a fuckin great game. This last play of HILT though completely soured all desire to pull out the game again. Figuring the new rules would shorten the game by about 15 minutes, I pulled it out thinking we'd be playing with 4, then 2 more people arrived and I didn't have the sense to say "oh yeah this is better at 4 for a learning game sorry" I said, yeah this is one of my favorite games we should keep adding more people. So basically this is all self-inflicted pain. If this were my first play of Condottiere, I'd have rated it a 5.0. Like I mentioned before, no subtle signaling or outrageous calls, the poker-like play completely gone. A waste of a great game...but not unlike my first play of the game. Condottiere is not for everyone.

My honest recommendation is that the game is best with new people only at 3 players, and not to go past 4 players unless everyone is fine with negotiation. And it's best when someone is getting backstabbed.
