played a game. it was a bad night for games in the first place. trump was just elected president, conversations were bad, the wrong team won, etc etc etc. our host just got a kickstarter game they wanted to play and they're the best person i know to play co-op dungeon crawlers with. so we break out  *Dungeons of Infinity*, a half-roleplaying co-op dungeon crawler where everyone picks a typical dnd class and tries to get loot and kill the big bad in board game form. first game went terribly. terribly as in, our valiant heroes were underprepared and overzealous of the difficulties of this dungeon. we died to a goop trap. it didn't help that our mage was terrified of straight hallways, our monk was lost in the tomes and tomes of rules, and our tank was looting somewhere behind us. they had a shiny new shield but nowhere in sight. and it was an epic shield btw. after the goop trap incident we quickly reset since that was about 30 minutes, and everyone stayed as the same characters with newfound purpose and strategy. except me, the tank that really wanted to loot. i've played games before, i understand what co-op and dungeon crawl means. I also know that sometimes sacrifices need to be made so i that can get a cool new pair of shoes. obviously, the teacher of the game was making very clear comments about our tank not tanking and had somehow aligned chaotic in the roll. great times.     

we were making great progress and by that i meant the rest of the team was fumbling through getting closer to the boss and i was fully suited up to take some major hits at the start of the dungeon. at some point, the goop trap once again came up but we were much more aware of the deadliness of goop. crisis averted. our thief was the first in turn order so they were taking charge and exploring new rooms for us. unfortunately for them, they were valiant and strong in heart, but not in body. they opened a door to a monster that caught you if you ran, and our boy was stuck without the ability to escape. the monster wasn't very threatening, but it was when it was in a room with 3 other men that wanted to do some killing. our team was only slightly behind and were able to catch up to help take blows, but targeting and the rules of the game make our young scout take low but consistent damage. ouchies. also unfortunately, they made the choice to keep taking hits instead of throwing aggro to another player by not attacking. they were signed to their doom and took lethal damage. another parody of a dungeon crawl was completed. in the usual, we discussed a bit about the play and our mishaps and we absolutely dumpstered me about my looting, the goop trap, and how i should have made it to the front. and i said yeah bro we sucked ass. our team makes no sense damn shouldn't have had the thief tank, why was our thief tanking in the first place bro? died so fast.     
    

i did a bad thing.    
    

the thief player immediately stood up and said:      

>"Just because you've played so many more games than I have so you have a lot more experience with these kinds of games. I know that I'm not as good at games as you but that does not mean you have to comment on the moves I make, ok? I was not having a bad time and I was fine with the game before you said anything."    

what am i doing in games bro i just say shit damn i don't even know why this is a blog this is just shit that happens man.
